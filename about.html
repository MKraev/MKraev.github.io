Profile

I lead the Project Management function within a global product organisation, running virtual delivery teams of 10–20 specialists across core banking migrations, card-scheme deliveries and regulatory compliance programmes. My strengths are delivery governance, resource and risk management, and translating stakeholder priorities into realistic, achievable plans within hybrid Agile environments.

In parallel I'm completing a PhD in Computer Science at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences, researching how agentic AI can make large data collections easier to search, understand and trust.
