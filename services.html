How an engagement works

  1. Discovery call

    A free 30-minute conversation about the goal, constraints and timeline.

  2. Proposal

    A short written scope with deliverables, milestones and a fixed or time-based price.

  3. Delivery

    Regular check-ins, transparent status reporting and early escalation of risks.

  4. Handover

    Documentation and knowledge transfer so your team can carry on independently.

Engagement models

Project-based

A defined scope and outcome — e.g. a migration plan, a requirements package or an AI proof of concept.

Advisory

A few hours a month as a sounding board for delivery leads, PMOs or product teams.

Workshops

Half-day or full-day sessions on delivery governance, risk management or AI assistants over your own data.
