Hi, I'm Milen. For more than ten years I've worked on technology programmes in banking, payments and fintech — first as a data analyst, then as a business analyst and product manager, and today as a team lead for project and product delivery. Alongside that I'm finishing a PhD at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences on **agentic AI in large data management systems**.

This site brings both worlds together. Here's what I plan to write about:

- **Delivery in practice** — planning, risk and dependency management, and how to keep commitments realistic when the portfolio is full.
- **Payments and fintech** — card-scheme deliveries, migrations and compliance work, from the delivery side.
- **Agentic AI research** — what it takes for an AI assistant to answer only from trusted sources, cite its evidence and say "I don't know" when it should.

If any of this is relevant to your work, I'd be glad to [hear from you]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).
