Projects

Thesis prototype

LibAssist — an agent-based assistant for digital libraries

An assistant that answers readers' questions only from the documents in the library. Every answer is structured: a short answer, claims linked to the cited passages, sources, a confidence level and stated limitations. When the collection doesn't contain enough evidence, the assistant declines honestly and points to the closest documents.

  • Hybrid retrieval over prepared library resources
  • Six specialised agents with dynamic orchestration and checkpoints
  • Library-independent: everything specific lives in a profile and adapters
  • Evaluation framework with a series of controlled experiments

Platform contribution

AI module for the CultIS platform

CultIS is a web platform for intelligent management and presentation of large datasets and knowledge in culture, the humanities and social sciences. I work on its AI module: a retrieval-augmented assistant that answers questions from the platform's own content index, with model providers that can be swapped.

  • RAG over the platform's content index
  • Pluggable local and cloud model providers
  • Research into reliability and domain limits of heritage chatbots
